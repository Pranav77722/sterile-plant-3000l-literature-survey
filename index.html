Section 1.0

1. Introduction

Theoretical grounding and engineering motivation behind the supervisory automation architecture.

This literature survey presents a comprehensive review of key research studies in industrial automation, programmable logic controllers (PLCs), Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) systems, automated Cleaning-In-Place (CIP)/Steam-In-Place (SIP) sequences, safety interlock design patterns, alarm management, and real-time process monitoring. The primary objective is to evaluate established engineering methodologies, control paradigms, communication protocols, and supervisory frameworks to form a robust technical foundation for the design, development, and implementation of a 3000 LTR fully automatic sterile manufacturing plant. By synthesizing findings across 10 foundational literature sources, this survey establishes the rationale behind selecting the Siemens S7-1200 CPU 1215C PLC, Zenon Supervisor 7.60 SCADA, Schneider ATV310 VFDs, Advantech IPC-615W Industrial PC, multi-parameter process instrumentation, and 21 CFR Part 11 compliant supervisory data management.

Decoupled Safety Interlocks

Section 3.0

3. Key Research Insights

Seven fundamental engineering conclusions derived from the reviewed publications that govern our system design.

01

Centralized Deterministic Control with Supervisory Authority

Literature consistently confirms that industrial PLCs (specifically Siemens S7-1200 platforms) provide reliable execution of critical process loops and deterministic control logic, while supervisory layers (SCADA/HMI) manage data acquisition, visualization, and user interactions without compromising controller authority.

Primary Sources: [1], [6], [9], [10] Target: Siemens S7-1200 1215C
02

Automated Multi-Stage Cleaning & Sterilization Logic

Research on Cleaning-In-Place (CIP) demonstrates that automating complex multi-step routines (pre-rinse, chemical washing, recirculation, WFI final rinse, SIP) reduces chemical/water consumption, ensures process repeatability, and provides conductivity-based validation essential for sterile manufacturing.

Primary Sources: [2] Target: CS01/CS02 & TS01–TS19
03

Formal Decoupling of Safety Guards and Functional Logic

Separating safety interlocks (as boolean guards on PLC outputs) from functional sequence execution ensures fail-safe protection across all operating modes (including pressure hold tests, SIP thermal holding, and emergency stops) independent of sequence or software errors.

Primary Sources: [3], [8] Target: Discrete Safety Guard Matrix
04

Actionable Alarm Rationalization (ANSI/ISA-18.2)

Applying ANSI/ISA-18.2 alarm rationalization principles prevents operator alarm flooding during transient events, ensuring that SCADA alarms remain informative, prioritized, and actionable with automated acoustic hooter, visual tower lamps, and contextual guidance.

Primary Sources: [7] Target: Zenon Alarm Matrix
05

Systematic I/O Sizing and Network Infrastructure

Decomposition of complex plants into modular subsystems provides a structured methodology for calculating analog/digital I/O counts, expansion module configuration, SCADA tag mapping, and robust Ethernet-based network topology design with industrial switches.

Primary Sources: [5] Target: 208 Valves + 34 Sensors
06

Continuous High-Frequency Data Acquisition & Logging

High-frequency database logging (e.g., 200 ms sampling intervals) enables real-time parameter tracking, historical trend generation, batch reporting, and audit trail retention crucial for 21 CFR Part 11 regulatory compliance and batch release.

Primary Sources: [4], [8] Target: 200ms SQL Archiving
07

Flexible Variable-Speed Motor Control

Utilizing PLC-controlled VFD outputs (such as Schneider ATV310 drives) allows precise closed-loop speed modulation for mixers and pumps, enhancing batch homogenization, preventing vortex foaming, and stabilizing sterile liquid transfer.

Primary Sources: [6], [9] Target: Schneider ATV310 (MM01/MM02, CFP)
Section 4.0

4. Research Gap and Project Motivation

Bridging the divide between fragmented academic prototypes and an industrial-grade 3000 LTR sterile production facility.

While individual studies in the reviewed literature address specific sub-domains of industrial automation—such as isolated motor speed control, standalone dairy CIP systems, palletizer safety guards, or generic SCADA interfaces—none provide a unified, fully integrated automation solution tailored specifically to the stringent requirements of a 3000 LTR Fully Automatic Sterile Manufacturing Plant.

Sterile liquid manufacturing requires seamless, concurrent integration of high-density instrumentation (19 temperature sensors TS01–TS19, 8 pressure transmitters PT01–PT08, pH01, CS01/CS02 conductivity sensors, LC03 load cells, FS01–FS03 flow switches), multi-actuator routing (208 pneumatic valves PV01–PV208), variable-speed agitation and pumping (Schneider ATV310 VFDs driving mixers MM01/MM02 and pumps CFP01–CFP03), multi-phase sequential automation (Pressure Hold Test, Steam-In-Place, Cleaning-In-Place, mixing, cooling, filling), and strict regulatory compliance (21 CFR Part 11 audit trails, electronic signatures, parameter logging, and historical reporting).

3000 LTR Sterile Plant Integrated Engineering Architecture

🌡️

Process Instrumentation (34+ Units)

  • TS01–TS19: 19 RTD Pt100 Sensors (Vessel, Jacket, Condensate)
  • PT01–PT08: 8 Sanitary Diaphragm Pressure Transmitters
  • pH01: In-Line Sanitary Glass pH Probe
  • CS01, CS02: Dual Toroidal Conductivity Sensors for WFI Purity
  • LC03: Vessel Tri-Mount Shear Beam Load Cells
  • FS01–FS03: Thermal Dispersion Flow Switches for Recirculation
🔧

Valve Actuation & Flow Routing

  • PV01–PV208: 208 Pneumatic Aseptic Diaphragm Valves
  • Fail-safe spring return (Normally Closed / Open) design
  • Decoupled Boolean output interlock guards (Riera et al.)
  • Festo / Bürkert Valve Manifold Islands via Profinet
  • Dual feedback reed switches for open/closed seating confirmation
⚙️

VFD Agitation & Transfer Pumps

  • Schneider ATV310: Modbus RTU / Profinet variable speed drives
  • MM01 / MM02: Main Vessel Bottom-Magnetic & Top Mixers
  • CFP01–CFP03: Sanitary Centrifugal Transfer & Recirculation Pumps
  • PID speed modulation prevents vortex cavitation & protein shearing
  • Real-time current, torque, and thermal overload supervision
💻

Supervisory Control & Compliance

  • Siemens S7-1200 CPU 1215C: Deterministic logic in TIA Portal V19
  • Advantech IPC-615W: Industrial grade rack PC (24/7 reliability)
  • Zenon Supervisor 7.60: High-end SCADA with animated P&IDs
  • FDA 21 CFR Part 11: Cryptographic audit trails, e-signatures
